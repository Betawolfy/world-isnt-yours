## Witch

> ***Cruel and cacking, Witches are masters of brewing, and are great with magic.***

## Powers :

### **Better Potion**
>   You consume potions better than most, Potions will last longer when you drink them.

### **Perfect Potion**
>   Get a random potion effect, based on the situation you are in.

### **Pillager Aligned**
>   Villagers don't like you, and pillagers like you!

## Tips :
- witches has the ability to get strangth very easily, so be careful when fighting them.

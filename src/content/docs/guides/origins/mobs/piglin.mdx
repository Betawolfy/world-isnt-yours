## Piglin

> ***Piglins are strange creatures of unknown origin, who formed their own society and culture worshipping their love of gold in the harsh wastelands of the Nether.***

## Powers :

### **Gold Worshipper**
> Your adoration for gold unlocks its hidden power,
> making golden armor unbreakable and as strong as diamond.

### **Bartering Master**
> You're brilliant at bartering after a lifetime of experience,
> everytime you barter, you get 2 and 5 times as many valuables.

### **Piglin Ally**
> Piglins don't attack you, unless you attack them first.

### **Sniper**
> Your aim is more accurate than humans.

### **Metamorphosis**
> You transform into a Zombified piglin if you're out of the Nether for too long.

### **Nether Inhabitant**
> Your natural spawn will be in the Nether.

## Powers recap : 
```diff
+ +100% durability for golden armor
+ +2 to 5 times more valuables from bartering
+ Piglins won't attack you
+ +100% bow/crossbow accuracy
- Transform into a Zombified piglin if out of the Nether for too long
- Natural spawn in the Nether
```

## Tips :
- Piglins won't attack you if you open chests in bastions.
- As a Zombified piglin:
    - Gold weapons will be more effective;
    - You lost hunger faster.
    - But, other zombified piglins will fight for you!
While in the nether, you can become a piglin again by using the villager curing process, (golden apple + potion of weakness) on yourself.

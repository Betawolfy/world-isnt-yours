## Zombie

> ***Zombies are the corpes of fallen builders that have reawakened in the world for unknown reasons.***

## Powers :

### Dark Sight
>    You can see in the dark when on land.

### Undead Ally
>    Undead mobs won't attack you, unless you attack them first.

### Photoallergic
>    You burn in daylight.

### Zombie Slowness
>    Your undead body moves at a slower pace than humans.

### Zombie Hunger
>    Your constant hunger for flesh makes you exhaust quicker than a human.

### Zombie Touch
>    You zombify villagers instead of killing them.

### Terrifying Monster
>    Villagers are scared of you and refuse to trade with you.

### Metamorphosis
>    You transform into a Husk if you're in the desert for too long,
>    a Drowned if you're in water for too long.

### Carnivore
>    Your diet is restricted to meat, you can't eat vegetables.

## Powers recap : 
```diff
+ Can see in the dark
+ Undead mobs won't attack you
- Burn in daylight
- Slower movement speed
- Exhaust quicker
- Zombifies villagers instead of killing them
- Villagers refuse to trade with you
- Transforms into a Husk in the desert and a Drowned in water
- Can't eat vegetables
```

### Tips :
- You should make your home underground, so you can see in the dark and avoid sunlight.
- This is the origins with the most drawbacks. Think carefully before choosing it.

## Wither Skeleton

> ***Locked away in the Nether, over time Wither Skeletons have become resistant to fire.***

## Powers :

### **Wither**
> Anything you hit gets the wither effect.

### **Undead Ally**
> Undead mobs won't attack you, unless you attack them first.

### **Skeletal Form**
> Your skeleton body means you have less health than humans.

### **Poor Digestion**
> You can only hold half as much saturation as humans.

### **Fire Immunity**
> You are immune to all type of fire damage.

### **Nether Inhabitant**
> Your natural spawn will be in the Nether.

## Powers recap : 
```diff
+ Wither effect on hit
+ Undead mobs won't attack you
+ Immune to fire damage
- Half the health of humans
- Half the saturation of humans
- Natural spawn in the Nether
```

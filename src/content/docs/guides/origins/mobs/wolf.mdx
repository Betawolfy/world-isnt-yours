## Wolf

> ***Strong and Fast, Wolves are creatures that have strength in numbers.***

## Powers :

### **Wolf body**
> You have 2 less hearts of health than humans.

### **Werewolf-like**
>   During a full moon, you get Faster, Stronger and Healthier.

### **Alpha Wolf**
>   Wolves you tame are stronger!

### **Howl**
>   You can use the left click key when holding nothing to howl,
>   and give speed and strength to nearby wolves and yourself.

### **Wolf Pack**
>   When you are near at least 4 wolves you gain speed and attack damage.

### **Carnivore**
> Your diet is resticted to meat, you can't eat vegetables.

## Powers recap : 
```diff
+ Faster, stronger and healthier during a full moon
+ Tamed wolves are stronger
+ Can howl to give speed and strength to nearby wolves and yourself
+ Gain speed and attack damage when near at least 4 wolves
- 2 less hearts of health
- Can't eat vegetables
```

### Tips :
- You should tame wolves to benefit from the Alpha Wolf power, and to have some companions.
- Howling before beginning a fight can give you and your wolves a great advantage.

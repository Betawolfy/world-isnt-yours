## Creeper

> ***A remnant of an unexpected mistake, the Creeper blasts it's way through its enemies.***

## Powers :

### **Creeper Ally**
> Creepers won't attack you.

### **Explosive**
> You can sacrifice some of your health to create an explosion every 15 seconds.

### **Afraid of Cats**
> You get nausea and slowness when around cats.

### **Unwieldy**
> This way your hands are formed provide no way of holding a shield upright.

## Powers recap : 
```diff
+ Creepers won't attack you
+ Detonation possibility every 15 seconds
- Detonateing costs health
- Nausea and slowness around cats
- Can't hold a shield upright
```

## Tips :
- If you don't wanna be alone, you can prioritize dogs over cats.
- Explosions can help to unlocks some hidden secrets. Better have a creeper with you in quests. :) 
- Be sure to use Protections enchantments correctly, as shield will be unusable for you.

## Evoker

> ***Master of magic, and a collector of totems.***

## Powers :

### **Summon Fangs**
>   You have the ability to summon fangs! 

### **Arcane Totem**
>   Totems have a 10% chance to not break on use.

### **Pillager Aligned**
>   Villagers don't like you, and pillagers like you!

## Tips :
- Fangs can easily scare people away, but takes long to recharge.
- Even tho the totem has a 10% chance to not break, still come with many totems.

## Fox

> ***Sly and cunning, foxes are timid animals who enjoy eating berries, and stealing items.***

## Powers :

### **Timid Creature**
>   Your speed increases when you are around more than 3 others players.

### **Better Berries**
>   Berries taste extra delicious to you! 

<Aside type="note" title="Clarifications about berries:"> 
    Berries provide more saturation and hunger.
</Aside>

### **Careful Gatherer**
>   Sweet Berries Bushes don't hurt you at all.

### **Item Collector**
>   You have a larger pickup radius.

### **Sly**
>   You are quicker.

## **Small Fox**
>   You have 2 less hearts of health than humans.

## Powers recap : 
```diff
+ Speed increases when around more than 3 players
+ Berries provide more saturation and hunger
+ Sweet Berries Bushes don't hurt you
+ Larger pickup radius
+ Quicker
- 2 less hearts of health
```

### Tips :
- You should play with friends, so you can benefit from the speed boost.
- Your large pickup radius makes you a great farmer or loot collector.

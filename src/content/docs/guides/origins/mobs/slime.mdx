## Slime

> ***Green blobs of Jello, that bounce all around!***

## Powers :

### **Bouncy**
>   All blocks act like slime blocks.

### **Split Ability**
>   Turn your food points into a small slime to defend you!

### **Small Weakness**
>   When at less than 2 hearts of health, you deal almost no damage,
>   BUT your attacks have stronger knockback!

### **Acrobatics**
>   Your never take fall damage, no matter from which height you fall.

## Powers recap : 
```diff
+ All blocks act like slime blocks
+ Can turn food points into a small slime to defend you
+ Never take fall damage
- Deal almost no damage when at less than 2 hearts of health
```

## Tips :
- The blocks bouncing can be an help for critically hitting someone.

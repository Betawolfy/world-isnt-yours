## Skeleton

> ***Reanimated bones of fallen archers, Skeletons have unlimited arrows and a perfect aim.***

## Powers :

### **Dark Sight***
> You can see in the dark when on land.

### **Infinite Arrows**
> Arrows you shoot are not used up.

### **Undead Ally**
> Undead mobs don't attack you, unless you attack them first.

### **Sniper**
> Your aim is more accurate than humans.

### **Photoallergic**
> You burn in sunlight.

### **Skeletal Form**
> Your skeleton body means you have less health than humans.

### **Poor Digestion**
> You can only hold half as much saturation as as a human.

### **Metamorphosis**
> You transform into a Stray if you're in the cold for too long.

## Powers recap : 
```diff
+ Night vision
+ Infinite arrows
+ Undead mobs won't attack you
+ +100% bow/crossbow accuracy
- Burn in sunlight
- Less health than humans
- Half saturation than humans
- Transform into a Stray if you're in the cold for too long
```

## Tips :
- As a stray:
    - You can transform back into a skeleton by staying in a warm biome like the desert for a long time.
    - All arrows you shoot inflict Slowness.
    - You take no damage from powder snow, but you move slower in warmer biomes.

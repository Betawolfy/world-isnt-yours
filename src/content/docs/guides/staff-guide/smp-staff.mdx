Hello, my silly staff. In this guide, I'll teach you some very interesting staff mechanisms and how to moderate efficiently on the SMP.

## I. Your permissions
You have the right to do the following things: 

<Aside type="note" title="Small clarification">

### Inventory related :
    - You are allowed to look at inventories.
    - Though you are not allowed to modify.

### Moderation related :
    - You are allowed to kick and ban.
    - You can also teleport to other players without using /tpa. 

### Anti-grief related :
    - You are allowed to inspect blocks that have been placed, destroyed, etc.
    - You can also look up containers (who took what item and who deposited items).
    - You cannot revert a grief.

### Whitelist related :
    - You are allowed to whitelist players.

### Sanity related:
    - You are allowed to look at other people's howIFeel.
    - You are as well allowed to either remove or add sanity to other players.

## III. Moderation

As a staff, you need to assure that rules are respected and that the server is a nice place to be in. Everyone is welcomed, but if someone is being toxic, you need to take action.

### 1. Warn system
There are not "official" warn system in the SMP, but if you feel a problem doesn't require a kick or more, do privately warn the player. You can do it by DMing them, or by using the command `/msg <nickname> <message>` in-game.

### 2. Kicking and banning
If the problem is more serious, you can kick or ban the player.

To kick a player, you can use the command: 
`/kick <nickname> <reason>`

To ban a player, you can use the command: 
`/ban <nickname> <reason>`

Be sure to ***not forget the `.`*** if the player is a Bedrock player.

<Aside type="caution" title="You can ban offline players">
    If, for x or y reason, the player disconnected, you can still ban them by using the same command as above. Just make sure to write the correct nickname.    
</Aside>

### 3. Teleporting
If a player has a problem or need help from a staff, you can teleport to them by using the command: 
`/tp <nickname>`

### 4. Griefing
If you see a grief, you can inspect the blocks and containers to find out who is the culprit. 

You can use the following commands:

`/co i` and clicking on a block to inspect it. you can also click on chest, barrels, etc. to see who took or deposited items.

`/co lookup time:<time> radius:<radius>` to see all the actions that happened in a certain area and time. recommendin to use it at *the time you check* ***minus*** *1 day* and a radius of 10 or 20 blocks.

<Aside type="note" title="Note about reverting">
    You cannot revert a grief, so if you see one, be sure to find the culprit and take action against them. A higher staff can then revert the grief, and, if not possible, compensate the victim.
</Aside>

### 5. Inventory checking
If you see a player doing something suspicious, you can check their inventory by using the command: 
`/invsee <nickname>`

If, for exemple, you see them with a lot of precious ore while mining, you can check if they are using x-ray.
    Or, if they have a excessive amount of creative-only items, they may be cheating or stealing.

***THOUGH!*** Admins can gift some creative items, but only test blocks or lights blocks; if a user has a command block, for exemple, it is a sign of stealing in a admin chest.

## IV. Sanity
As staff, you are allowed to 'mess' with other people's sanity. 
You can either add or remove sanity to other players, and you can also look at their howIFeel.

### 1. Looking at howIFeel

In order to look at someone's howIFeel, you can either do that:

When someone sends a howIFeel, it will be displayed: 
    - One, for all staff online (or people with the `de.howifeel.view` permission) in the game chat.
    - Two, in the #howifeel channel in the discord.

### 2. Adding or removing sanity

To edit someone's sanity, you can use the following commands: 

`/sanity add <nickname> <amount> <reason>` to add sanity.

`/sanity remove <nickname> <amount> <reason>` to remove sanity.

### 3. How to choose the amount of sanity to add or remove?

Here is the amount to add/remove depending the context:

+20 (or -20) if this is family related '(e.g. "I found my papa again!!" worth +20 points, while "PAPAAAAA WHERE ARE YOU..." worth -20 points).'

+10 (or -10) if this is a good (or bad) moment in the SMP '(e.g. "I found a new friend!" worth +10 points, while "I lost all my items..." worth -10 points).'

+5 (or -5) if this is a neutral moment in the SMP '(e.g "I built a new house!" worth +5 points, while "I got lost..." worth -5 points).'

*Note that this is stackable. meaning that if someone says "I found a new friend and I lost all my items", you can do math:  +10-20, which will give a total of -10 points.*

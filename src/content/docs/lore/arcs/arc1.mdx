## ==[[ARC 1: THE SERVER]]==

During the times people have played in this server, there was a random server announcement that occurs every now and then in which its outputs provided are.. odd. Instead of typical error messages or announcements from the admins, it speaks as if the server itself is conscious. This is what people named as the " Server ". To differ, this entity has its name colored in purple upon sending a message, and instead formatted as " [Server] " instead of " (Server) ". The way this entity speaks is rather concerning, for sometimes it targets players with its message, making it sound like a threat or a concern.

After Kairus lost to a bet made by Lucy whereas he must survive a week as another origin (where the origin in mention is a Merling) and avoid dying 5 times during it, there was a strange crater that randomly spawned right beside Spawn. This crater was deep and corrupted—similar in design as to what you see at Spawn within its lands and the claws supporting the crystal floating above it all. As some people traversed deeper into the crater, there lies a chest containing three papers:

*" ????????????? "*
*" Qpna vjg dgikppki. "* (" Only The Beginning. ")
*" (2 a -> c) "*

After decoding the second paper and heading back up, some who remained has found few more papers and collected them. Although one has gone missing, the remaining papers found reads as follows:

*" fr/consequences.html "*
*" : ) "*
*" Only one."*
*" Something will be here. "*
*" Only one."*

Additionally, Lucy found an extra location 80,000 blocks away. Located in a desert biome—there is an "island" of corruption sitting atop of the sand, and in the middle of it sits a lectern with a book. The book was meant to be the Server's message to Lucy where it states the following:

*" My dear betawolfy_,*
*Your little attempts to silence me will never work.*

*I am everywhere.*
*I can do anything.* 
*I made everything here.*

*You do not own this world, you will never own this world.* 

*This does not only apply to you, but to everyone, posing blocks to claim their 'homes.'* 

*I'll give you some time. Choose your next actions wisely.*
*— [REDACTED] : ) "* 

 Everyone became suspicious of this message immediately. While everyone was focused on the message, Cyber then decided to check the link provided—there lies three `.txt` files which can be opened at the website's terminal. These are `consequences.txt`, `coords.txt`, and `logs.txt`. 

Immediately after checking the `.txt` files, hell rained loose at Spawn. The players fought the Skeleton King that spawned from the Server in order to destroy them, which everyone beats it with ease. After a while of fighting, The Server summoned a wither as a last attempt to destroy them—which was also a piece of cake to the masses. 

The fights never stopped there, for the longest of time there has been threats being sent at Spawn where the players must clear it in order to defend their world. Sometimes things have gone crucial to the point the beacons securing the crystal turned pitch black as a sign of danger—yet nothing has broke the spirits of these players down.

With battles won in victory,
comes with more threats incoming. 

### TL;DR:

*The Server is an entity that mimics itself as a server announcement, except its name is colored in purple whereas it brings threats within the members of the server. After a losing bet, a crater spawned with named papers implying about an upcoming doom. Additionally, there was a corrupted island on a desert 80,000 blocks away with a letter designated for Lucy. Shortly after, hell arises with The Server spawning bosses left and right, however the players beat them with ease.*

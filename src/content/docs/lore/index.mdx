***Welcome to the lore center!***
_New to the server? need lore refresh? or just want to know more about the server's story?_

The lore center repertoriates all known events and summarize them in a way that is easy to understand. 
It also provides a timeline of events and a list of characters that are involved in the story.

Lore is divided and explained through many arcs, and each arc has its own story and events that happen in it. 
You can find the arcs in the "Arcs" section of the lore center.

Through the entire story, the players are ***more than welcome*** to contribute to the lore by sharing their experiences, theories, and ideas about the server's story.
We are also open to any additions. simply open a ticket on the server or reach out to the admins if you have any ideas or suggestions for the lore.
